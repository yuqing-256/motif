Motif supports two appearance modes:

- **Motif** — the light appearance and initial visual foundation.
- **Motif Dark** — an independently designed dark appearance derived from Motif.

## Motif Dark is independently designed

Motif Dark is not produced through simple color inversion. It requires its own design decisions,
visual adjustments, and validation, while preserving the underlying design principles, visual
identity, and component semantics of Motif.

## Independent of the design approaches

The two appearance modes are independent of the Hugin and Munin design approaches. Neither approach
is tied to a particular appearance mode.

Both Hugin and Munin must work coherently in Motif and Motif Dark. The two approaches may also
coexist within the same product or page in either appearance mode.

## Not a density dimension

No independent Density dimension is defined in the initial system. Other appearance or presentation
dimensions should be introduced only when demonstrated requirements justify them.
