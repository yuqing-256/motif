Motif initially defines four independent themes. They are four complete themes, not combinations
generated from independent density, brightness, or other theme dimensions. No independent Density
dimension is defined.

## Hugin and Munin as approaches

Hugin and Munin are complementary design approaches as well as the names of the two core themes.
They are not separate component libraries or mutually exclusive interface modes, and both may
contribute to the same product or page.

## Hugin

Hugin is Motif's editorial design approach. It focuses on content expression, presentation,
reading, and communication:

- Content hierarchy and narrative structure.
- Typography and reading experience.
- Visual emphasis and information presentation.

## Munin

Munin is Motif's functional design approach. It focuses on content creation, organization,
management, and operation:

- Information architecture and navigation.
- Workspaces and operational workflows.
- Controls and interaction states.

## Hugin Dark

Hugin Dark is an independently designed dark derivative of Hugin. It may share principles and
selected foundations with Hugin, but it requires its own design decisions and validation.

## Munin Light

Munin Light is an independently designed light derivative of Munin. It may share principles and
selected foundations with Munin, but it requires its own design decisions and validation.

## What the themes are not

The themes are not a switch between two visual styles, and they must not be derived through simple
color inversion. Theme implementation must preserve each theme's design purpose and allow
independent validation.
